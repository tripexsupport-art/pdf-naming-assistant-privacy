PDF Naming Assistant — Privacy Notice

Last updated: October 17, 2026

This notice covers manual review, optional customer-owned AI connections and Gumroad purchase activation.

Documents and local reviews

The application reads PDFs you select and saves review data on your computer. It has no built-in account, telemetry or vendor-hosted AI. Original PDFs remain in place. Exported copies and manifests are written to your chosen destination.

Reviews can contain original file paths, extracted document text, date excerpts, proposed titles and your decisions. They are not encrypted by the app. Local data is normally stored under %LOCALAPPDATA%\PDFNamingAssistant. The app's Storage & privacy control shows the location.

Your API connection

AI suggestions are optional. If you choose your own API and confirm transmission, extracted text and date excerpts go directly to your selected provider with the model request. The original PDF file is not uploaded by the app. Your provider's charges, retention and privacy terms apply. The app supplies no API key or credits. Cancellation cannot undo a request already received by the provider.

Your key stays in memory by default. If you opt into saving it, Windows DPAPI encrypts it for your current Windows account. Someone or software running with access to that account may still access protected data. Use Session → Forget the current API key to remove the saved key for the current connection. Revoke compromised keys at your provider.

Local models

Local mode connects to a loopback server on your computer. You supply its server software and model files. Those components have their own behavior and licenses.

Trial and purchase records

The access subfolder contains the trial ledger and purchase activation. The ledger records export paths, content hashes and outcomes to account for successful or interrupted copies; it does not contain extracted PDF text or API keys.

When you confirm Gumroad purchase activation, the app sends your purchase key and this app's product ID to https://api.gumroad.com/v2/licenses/verify. It does not send PDFs, extracted text or AI API keys. Gumroad may return purchase details; this app retains only the purchase key, product ID and sale ID in Windows-encrypted local storage, not returned email or card details. Subsequent normal use works offline without a Gumroad request on every launch. Optional seller-issued signed-file activation is offline. Buying through Gumroad is separate and subject to Gumroad's privacy and payment practices.

Deletion and retention

Session → Delete this saved review removes that review, not original PDFs, exports, other reviews, trial records, backups or provider-held requests. To remove all local app data, close the app and remove its local storage folder. Remove exports separately from your chosen destinations. Keep the original seller-issued purchase license if you plan to reinstall.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact tripexsupport@gmail.com. Do not send API keys or confidential PDF content in support requests.
